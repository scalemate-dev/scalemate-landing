## What to look for before comparing tools

Most listicles jump straight into rankings. Before that, here's what actually matters when you're choosing a bulk ad launcher — the criteria we'll use to compare everything below.

**Flexibility.** Every team structures campaigns differently. Some want one ad per ad set for isolated testing. Others group 10 creatives under one campaign and split by geo or language. Two questions: does the tool force its own structure, or can you define yours? And can you save templates and reuse them — or do you rebuild from scratch every time? Also worth asking: if you need something specific (custom platform, ad grouping, naming conventions, a workflow that matches how your team operates), can the tool's team build it for you?

**Platform support.** Meta only, or Meta and TikTok? If your team runs campaigns across both, a Meta-only tool means repeating the process on TikTok's side manually.

**Integration into your stack.** Think long-term. Can this tool connect to your existing analytics, BI tools, or internal dashboards through an API? Or is it a standalone app you'll need to switch to every time you launch? Even if your volume is small now, migrating from one tool to another later costs weeks of setup and retraining. Picking a tool with an open API and integration options means you're not locked into someone else's workflow as you scale.

Where do your creatives live? If the answer is Google Drive, Airtable, or an internal pipeline, check whether the tool can pull from there directly. The download-reupload cycle is where most time gets wasted. Some tools only accept local file uploads. Others sync server-to-server.

Once ads are live, what happens? Some tools stop at launch. Others manage the campaigns after — automation rules that pause underperformers, scale winners, adjust budgets. The difference between "launching ads" and "running campaigns" is whether the tool stays involved after day one.

**Meta verification.** There's a wave of free AI-powered scripts and open-source tools that promise to automate ad launches. Be careful. If they connect to Meta through unverified API access or your personal access token, your ad account can be permanently banned. Meta flags these as bot activity. We covered this in detail in our [MCP servers guide](/blog/best-mcp-servers-meta-google-ads). Before connecting anything to your accounts, check if the tool passed Meta's official app review.

**Pricing transparency.** Some tools show pricing on their site. Others make you book a demo to find out. We'll include every price we can confirm.

### 2. AdManage.ai

AdManage positions itself as a high-volume bulk launcher. It handles Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, Snapchat, Pinterest, and more. Cloud sync from Google Drive, Dropbox, Frame.io, Air.inc, and Box — server-to-server, no local downloads.

- **Platforms:** Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, Snapchat, Pinterest, Taboola
- **Pricing:** ~$700/month
- **Templates:** Yes
- **Creative source:** Google Drive, Dropbox, Frame.io, Air.inc, Box
- **Post-launch:** Basic rules
- **Meta verified:** Yes

**Best for:** Agencies and large teams with budget, running across many platforms, who need the widest cloud storage and platform support. The price reflects enterprise-level scope.

### 3. Ads Uploader

Badged Meta Business Partner focused on bulk ad creation. Clean interface, flat pricing, Google Drive integration included.

- **Platforms:** Meta only
- **Pricing:** $59/month flat (unlimited accounts, uploads, ad spend). 7-day free trial
- **Templates:** Yes — Campaign Presets with dynamic naming patterns
- **Creative source:** Google Drive + local upload
- **Post-launch:** Ad scheduling, but no automation rules
- **Meta verified:** Yes (Meta Business Partner)

**Best for:** Teams running Meta-only campaigns who want simple bulk launching with Google Drive sync at a flat price.

### 4. Birch (formerly Revealbot)

Birch is primarily a rules and automation platform, not a bulk launcher. But its automation capabilities make it relevant for teams who need post-launch management across platforms.

- **Platforms:** Meta, Google, TikTok, Snapchat
- **Pricing:** From $49/month
- **Templates:** No bulk launch templates
- **Creative source:** Local upload
- **Post-launch:** Strong automation rules across platforms
- **Meta verified:** Yes

**Best for:** Teams where the bottleneck is managing live campaigns (pausing, scaling, bid adjustments), not launching them. Cross-platform rules are Birch's real strength.

### 5. kitchn.io

Bulk ad creation tool for Meta with cloud storage sync. Upload creatives directly from Google Drive or Dropbox, apply default settings across batches, and publish fast.

- **Platforms:** Meta only
- **Pricing:** On request (demo required)
- **Templates:** Default settings for batch consistency
- **Creative source:** Google Drive + Dropbox (server-to-server, no downloads)
- **Post-launch:** Limited
- **Meta verified:** Unclear

**Best for:** Teams who want cloud-synced bulk uploads on Meta with standardized workflows and naming conventions.

### 6. AdStellar AI

AI-powered platform that combines bulk ad launching with creative generation. Creates image ads, video ads, and UGC-style avatar content. AI campaign builder structures campaigns from historical performance data.

- **Platforms:** Meta only
- **Pricing:** 7-day free trial, paid plans (pricing on request)
- **Templates:** 50+ trending video templates + AI campaign builder
- **Creative source:** Local upload (no cloud storage sync)
- **Post-launch:** Performance ranking, AI optimization
- **Meta verified:** Unclear

**Best for:** Teams who want AI creative generation AND bulk launching in one tool on Meta. Stronger on the creative production side than most launchers.

### 7. Adnova

Platform that combines creative analytics, AI ad templates, and a bulk ad launcher. Claims to launch 100+ ads in a single click. Also offers 1000+ Canva and Figma ad templates.

- **Platforms:** Meta (Google Ads support unclear)
- **Pricing:** Bulk Launcher from $300/month. Other products $500-$20K/month
- **Templates:** 1000+ Canva/Figma ad templates + mix-and-match creatives
- **Creative source:** Local upload (no cloud storage sync mentioned)
- **Post-launch:** Creative analytics included
- **Meta verified:** Unclear

**Best for:** Teams who want creative analytics + ad templates + bulk launching bundled together. Higher price point reflects broader product suite beyond just launching.

### 8. Ad Mule

Google Sheets-based bulk ad creator for Meta. Similar concept to Markifact but with a different workflow.

- **Platforms:** Meta only
- **Pricing:** Paid
- **Templates:** Google Sheets
- **Creative source:** Sheets-based
- **Post-launch:** No automation
- **Meta verified:** No (Sheets-based, doesn't connect via app)

**Best for:** Teams who want to stay in Google Sheets and only run Meta campaigns.

### 9. Madgicx

AI-powered Meta optimization platform with a built-in Ad Launcher. Primarily known for AI audiences and creative scoring, but the Ad Launcher pushes ads into existing ad sets.

- **Platforms:** Meta only
- **Pricing:** From $55/month (scales with ad spend)
- **Templates:** No reusable launch templates
- **Creative source:** Upload to Madgicx first (no cloud sync)
- **Post-launch:** Automation rules + AI optimization
- **Meta verified:** Yes

**Best for:** Teams who want AI-driven optimization on Meta and occasionally need to push new ads into existing campaigns. Not built for high-volume bulk launching. [Full Madgicx review →](/blog/madgicx-review-alternative)

### 10. Meta Ads Manager (native)

The free default. No bulk launch capability — each ad is created individually through the Ads Manager UI. Bulk import via Excel handles campaign structure but not creative files.

- **Platforms:** Meta only
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Templates:** No
- **Creative source:** Manual drag-and-drop
- **Post-launch:** Basic automated rules (250 limit per account)
- **Meta verified:** It IS Meta

**Best for:** Teams running fewer than 10 ads per week on a single account. Beyond that, the manual process becomes the bottleneck.

## How to choose

**Single account, low volume (<20 ads/week).** Meta native or Scalemate free tier. No reason to pay.

**Multi-platform (Meta + TikTok).** Scalemate (free start) or AdManage ($700/mo). kitchn.io also supports TikTok but pricing is opaque.

**Rules and automation priority.** Birch. It's not a bulk launcher, but its cross-platform automation rules are the strongest on this list.

**AI-powered optimization on Meta.** Madgicx for AI audiences and creative scoring. Not for bulk launching specifically.

**Enterprise, high volume, Meta only.** AdManage or Ads Uploader. The price difference is significant — evaluate whether the extra features justify $700 vs $59.

**Spreadsheet-native teams.** kitchn.io or Ad Mule. Both let you work in familiar interfaces.
