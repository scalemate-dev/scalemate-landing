### Root-cause analysis: why R1 changed from revert → title test

**Original review-agent decision (2026-05-04):** Revert title/meta to pre-Apr 19 baseline (pos collapsed 5.8 → 19.4 after Apr 20 edit).

**Re-evaluation:** Revert assumed position recovery = click recovery. GSC data disproves this — both versions have 0 clicks:

| Period | Position | Impressions (28d) | Clicks | CTR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-Apr 19 (original title) | 5.8 | ~1,500 | 0 | 0% |
| Post-Apr 20 (current title) | 19.5 | ~3,045 | 0 | 0% |

Position is not the blocker — page-level positioning is.

**SERP audit (live 2026-05-04, queries: `ad testing software`, `best ad testing tools 2026`):** Top-9 organic is fragmented across categories — analytics (Motion, Admetrics), creation/automation (Smartly.io, AdCreative.ai, **Madgicx**, AdsGo), split-testing platforms (Optimizely, VWO, Google Ads Experiments), consumer research (Quantilope, Neurons). Madgicx + Smartly appear in top-3 of multiple listicles — this is Scalemate's actual peer set.

**Differentiators for Scalemate vs peer set (Madgicx $69/mo, Smartly enterprise, AdsGo, AdEspresso):**
1. Free positioning (peers paid/freemium)
2. Multi-platform (Meta + TikTok) vs Meta-only competitors
3. 2M+ ads launched concrete proof point

**Decision:** Replace revert with title-test focused on multi-platform ICP angle. If the 2h test still yields 0 clicks at T+14d (2026-05-18), escalate to S3 (full content rework — likely process-article hybrid).
