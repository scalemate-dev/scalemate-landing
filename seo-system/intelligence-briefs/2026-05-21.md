## 🚨 Critical Findings (this cycle's headline)

### 1. Videos NOT indexed under scalemate.co domain (root-cause identified)

**Symptom (user-reported):** scalemate.co videos not appearing in Google video search.

**Diagnostic data:**
- `GSC search_type=video` for last 28d → **0 rows** (no pages getting video impressions)
- `site:scalemate.co` in Google Videos engine → **0 results**
- Query `scalemate automation rules` in Google Videos → demo video R6gn5zSILwM appears, but attributed to **youtube.com**, not scalemate.co

**Root cause:** the two video-bearing pages (`/features/automation-rules`, `/features/bulk-launch`) embed YouTube iframes and ship `VideoObject` JSON-LD where `contentUrl` and `embedUrl` both point to `youtube.com`. With no self-hosted video file and no video sitemap, Google attributes the video to the YouTube channel — the page itself is NOT eligible for video rich results / video search inclusion. Ahrefs confirms: both `/features/automation-rules` and `/features/bulk-launch` are flagged with **"Structured data has Google rich results validation error"** (issue id `b35de3ae...`, 5 pages affected total).

**Affected pages with rich-results errors:**
1. `/features/automation-rules` (has `VideoObject`)
2. `/features/bulk-launch` (has `VideoObject`)
3. `/solutions/media-buyers` (no video — error is on `SoftwareApplication` / `FAQPage`)
4. `/ad-creative-uploader` (no video — same)
5. `/blog/best-facebook-ads-automation-tools` (Article schema)

**Fix path (cheap → expensive):**
- **Cheap (D1):** add a `sitemap-videos.xml` with `<video:player_loc>` for the YouTube embeds. Submit to GSC. This MAY help Google associate the video with scalemate.co even though it's YouTube-hosted (not guaranteed; mixed results historically).
- **Expensive (R1 rework):** self-host short demo videos as `.mp4` on `scalemate.co/videos/...`, update `contentUrl` to that path. Video rich-snippet eligibility goes way up but engineering cost is real (encoding, CDN, fallback for slow connections).
- **Diagnostic step first:** run GSC URL Inspection on `/features/automation-rules` → check "Video indexing" panel. That tells us *what specifically* Google sees wrong (e.g., "video missing thumbnail", "video not crawlable") before committing to either fix path.

**Schema validation deep-dive (separate from video issue but in same error bucket):**

Three non-video pages also fail rich results validation. Likely culprit: `SoftwareApplication` schema missing required field. Need to run Google's Rich Results Test on each URL to confirm. Common gotchas: missing `aggregateRating`, malformed `offers.price`, or missing `applicationCategory`. Cheap to fix once we have the actual validation message.

**This is now a pattern, not an isolated incident** — two long-form thin-traffic blog posts that Google has decided not to index even with sitemap inclusion. Possible causes (need root-cause investigation, F1 in action list):

- Topical authority gap (other site sections more relevant, these orphan-ish)

**Recommended action:**
- Check GSC URL Inspection for both URLs — what status does Google report? "Discovered — not indexed" vs "Crawled — not indexed" vs "Excluded" tells us the diagnosis.
- If "Crawled — not indexed" → content quality issue, consider deprecation (301 to listicle for facebook-ads-automation, archive or rework facebook-automated-rules).
- If "Discovered — not indexed" → crawl-priority issue, internal-link boost from indexed pages may help.
