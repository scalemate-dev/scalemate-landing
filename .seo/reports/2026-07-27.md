### 🚨 Headline finding: blog-wide impression decline vs. growing product pages

This is the most important finding this cycle and reframes several items the 2026-07-25 report tracked as isolated "watch" cases. Filtering to all pages with ≥30 prior-window impressions and sorting by impression delta:

| Page type | Pages showing >20% impression **decline** | Pages showing >20% impression **growth** |
|---|---|---|
| `/blog/*` (10 posts) | 9 | 0 |
| Product/feature/use-case + home (8 pages) | 1 (`/pricing`, -74%, low absolute volume) | 6 |

Worst blog declines: `creative-testing-framework` -95% (56→3 imp), `adcreative-ai-alternative` -83% (231→40), `madgicx-review-alternative` -70% (1,589→473), `meta-andromeda-update` -65% (187→65), `bulk-upload-creatives-meta-tiktok` -63% (318→119), `best-bulk-ad-launch-tools` -60% (559→221), `best-mcp-servers-meta-google-ads` -33% (2,908→1,947), `best-ad-testing-tools` -28% (2,428→1,756).

Biggest product-page gains in the same window: `/ad-creative-uploader` +104% (481→983 imp, clicks 9→17), `/use-cases/ad-campaign-automation-rules` +88%, `/use-cases/scale-ad-campaigns-faster` +69%, `/creative-testing-framework-library` +40%, `/features/bulk-launch` +35%, homepage +32% (1,467→1,936 imp, clicks 29→41).

Site-wide daily trend (60d, `--trend`) shows **no** corresponding site-wide impression collapse — daily impressions bounce 300–900 throughout, actually mildly higher in July than late May. So this is a **mix-shift, not a traffic loss**: query share is moving from blog content to product/use-case/feature pages, not leaving the site.

**Why, with evidence:** cross-referencing against the internal-linking pull (§4) — every declining blog post has 2-4 inbound internal links; every growing product/use-case/feature page has 27-29. This is consistent with Google reducing blog-post visibility in favor of better-linked, higher-authority pages that cover overlapping intent (e.g. `bulk-upload-creatives-meta-tiktok` blog post down 63% while `/ad-creative-uploader` — its closest product-page equivalent — is up 104%). Not proven causally from this data alone, but the correlation is clean enough to act on cheaply (link equity is a fixable lever) before considering content rework.

Two corrections to 2026-07-25's tracking: (1) `adcreative-ai-alternative` was recorded with a blank prior-window value that run (read as "flat/new"); it's actually -83%. (2) The "06-19 SERP-softening event, isolated to a few posts" hypothesis from 2026-07-24/25 is superseded — it's blog-wide, not a handful of posts.
